About

A free place to list Skyrim mods, run by the community with no paid tiers and no ads.

How it works

This site doesn't store mod files. Each author publishes their mod as a release on their own GitHub repository, and the download buttons here link straight to that release. Screenshots are hosted by GitHub too.

Authors list their mods with a short form, and it's checked automatically: there's no waiting for approval. Only the owner of a GitHub repository can list or change its mod.

The pages themselves are static files on GitHub Pages, rebuilt by GitHub Actions whenever a mod is added or changed, and within minutes of an author publishing a new release. Download counts and stars are refreshed live, through a small free Cloudflare service, so they're never more than a few minutes old. That's how the whole site runs at no cost.

Virus scans, and why it's your call

Each author picks how their files are checked before they can be downloaded here. Every mod page shows which one was used.

Private scan (the default)
The file is scanned by ClamAV, an open-source antivirus, inside GitHub's own build system. At the same time its fingerprint (not the file) is looked up on VirusTotal, so if any of its engines already knows the file as malware, it's caught. The file never leaves GitHub. It's less likely to catch brand-new malware that nobody has reported yet.
Checked by VirusTotal (full scan)
The file is checked by VirusTotal's 70+ antivirus engines. This is the strongest check. If VirusTotal hasn't seen the file before, it's uploaded there, and VirusTotal's paid users (security companies) can access uploaded files.
Not virus-scanned
Nothing was checked, because the author chose no scan or the file is over VirusTotal's 650 MB limit. These mods carry a red warning on their card and page.
No scan is a guarantee. Antivirus engines miss new threats, and none of them can tell whether a mod will break your game or your saves. Mods here aren't reviewed by hand, so whether to trust a mod and its author is up to you. Prefer authors you know, read the description and comments, check the file's SHA-256 matches the one shown, and when in doubt scan it with your own antivirus first.

Safety

  • A new version appears only after its scan. Until then, the mod page shows a notice that it's on its way.
  • Each file's SHA-256 hash is recorded and shown on the Files tab, so you can check your download matches.
  • If an author replaces a file, it's scanned again. A file flagged by the scan hides the whole mod.
  • An author's display name is always shown next to their GitHub account, so nobody can pass themselves off as someone else.
  • Mods aren't reviewed by hand before they're listed, so rule breaks are dealt with through reports. Anyone can report a mod from its page.

Takedowns and reports

If your work was uploaded without your permission, or a mod breaks the rules, open a report. Mods that break the rules are removed.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking and no cookies. While you browse, your browser loads the pages from GitHub Pages, screenshots from GitHub, and live download counts from the site's Cloudflare service, or from GitHub directly if that's unavailable. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser only.

Questions

Does it work with mod managers?
Yes. Every mod page has a Download with MO2 / Vortex button. It needs the free Waypoint helper, a one-time setup, and then sends the file straight to your mod manager's downloads, checked against Waypoint's records. Without the helper, use the normal Download button and drag the archive into Mod Organizer 2's Downloads tab or onto Vortex's Mods page.
Why do I need a GitHub account?
Only authors need one. Downloading needs no account at all.
I added or edited a mod. Why don't I see it yet?
It usually shows 2 to 5 minutes after the bot replies, once the site has rebuilt and the files are scanned. Files VirusTotal hasn't seen before take a few minutes longer while it analyses them. Browsers also keep an old copy of the page for up to 10 minutes, so press Ctrl + F5 to reload.
Why isn't the newest version here yet?
The site notices a new release within a minute and lists it once its files have been virus-scanned, usually 2 to 5 minutes after it's published. The mod page tells you when one is waiting.
What happens if an author deletes their repository?
The mod page says so straight away and its downloads are switched off. It disappears from the lists at once, and from the site at the next update.
What do "AI-assisted" and "AI-generated" mean?
Every author says whether they used AI. AI-assisted means AI helped, for example with code or touch-ups, but the work is mainly theirs. AI-generated means a large part, such as voices, textures or models, was made by AI. The mod's Permissions tab lists which parts, and Browse can filter by it. It's the author's own statement; if it looks wrong, report the mod.
Can I reuse parts of a mod in my own?
Check the mod's Permissions tab. Each author says whether others may upload, modify, convert or reuse their work, and whether to ask first. When it's not stated, ask the author.
How do I endorse a mod?
Star its GitHub repository. The star count is shown on the mod page.
Is this connected to Nexus Mods?
No. It's an independent, non-commercial project.
